# Proposal: Custom Retail & Multi-Channel ERP Solution

**Prepared for:** Houston Fragrance Business (Client)
**Date:** February 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
This proposal outlines the development of a centralized Custom ERP Solution designed specifically for your multi-channel fragrance business. The system will unify your physical Houston retail store (via a custom POS) with your online sales channels (Shopify, TikTok Shop, and eBay) into a single, real-time "Source of Truth" for inventory, pricing, and accounting.

---

## 2. Project Scope & Module-Wise Timeline

**Total Estimated Timeline:** 2.5 Months (10 Weeks)

* **Phase 1: Core Architecture & Master Inventory (Weeks 1-2)**
  * Database setup and SKU standardization.
  * Centralized master inventory module (bulk CSV import/export).

* **Phase 2: Retail Point of Sale (POS) (Weeks 3-4)**
  * Cashier-friendly interface with barcode scanner integration.
  * Automated local tax calculations and receipt generation.

* **Phase 3: Multi-Channel API Integrations (Weeks 5-7)**
  * Real-time stock syncing across Shopify, TikTok Shop, and eBay.
  * Centralized order management from all platforms.

* **Phase 4: Pricing Tiers & Vendor Management (Weeks 8-9)**
  * Wholesale vs. Retail pricing groups.
  * Purchase Order (PO) creation, tracking, and supplier management.

* **Phase 5: Managerial Accounting & Launch (Week 10)**
  * Revenue, COGS, and Margin reporting dashboards.
  * System testing, staff training, and deployment.

## 4. Odoo Enterprise Alternative

For full transparency, an alternative approach is using a pre-built corporate system like Odoo Enterprise. While Odoo is a robust tool, it comes with a recurring cost model and a generic, complex user interface.

* **Implementation Fee:** Comparable setup/consulting fees to configure standard modules.
* **Recurring User Licenses (The Catch):** ~$25 - $35 per user, per month. 
  * *For a team of 8-10 staff members, you will pay Odoo **~$2,500 to $3,500+ every single year, forever**.*
* **Third-Party Plugins:** Odoo does not natively support all modern social commerce. It often requires purchasing 3rd-party marketplace plugins (costing hundreds of dollars each) to connect to channels like TikTok Shop.
* **User Experience:** The interface is built for generic corporate use. It is cluttered with unused features, requiring significantly more training for your retail cashiers compared to a custom-built, streamlined POS.

## 5. Software Capabilities & Extended Benefits

By choosing a custom architecture, you are investing in a system that works exactly the way you do, with the flexibility to grow as your business scales.

**Immediate Advantages:**

* **Zero License Fees:** Total ownership of the software means your technology costs stay flat even as you hire new employees.
* **Frictionless User Experience:** The Retail POS is designed specifically for high-speed checkout. Cashiers will require minimal training to use it.
* **Real-Time Synchronization:** Prevent overselling. A bottle sold in Houston instantly vanishes from your TikTok Shop and Shopify.
* **Wholesale Automation:** VIP customers get automatic bulk pricing applied without staff having to remember manual discounts.

**Future Extensibility (What you can add later):**

Because you own the system, you are never locked into a vendor's roadmap. In the future, this system can easily be expanded to include:

* **AI Demand Forecasting:** Predict which fragrances will sell out next month based on historical data.
* **B2B Wholesale Portal:** A dedicated login portal where your wholesale clients can place bulk orders themselves.
* **Multi-Store Support:** Opening a second retail location? The system can easily segment inventory between "Warehouse A" and "Warehouse B".
* **Loyalty & Rewards Program:** Automatically track customer points across both the physical store and your online Shopify store.
